  • You are in control. You can delete saved data in the app, reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalization on your device.

1. Who is responsible

Travel Wizard (“the app”, “I”, “me”) is developed and published by Sattar Rahimbayli, based in Germany. For questions about this policy, write to sattar.rahimbayli@gmail.com.

Travel Wizard is a trip-planning app. You enter a destination and a trip length, and the app creates an itinerary and shows tools such as weather, a currency converter, a phrasebook, local emergency numbers and a chat guide for each place.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following in a local database on your phone. This data is not uploaded to me and I cannot see it.

  • Your name, as you typed it on the welcome screen. It is only used to greet you.
  • Preferences: temperature unit and home currency.
  • Trips: country, city, number of days, the itinerary (places, addresses, descriptions, prices) and photo links.
  • Chats: the questions you ask the place guide and the answers you receive.
  • Saved copies of downloaded information so the app works offline: place guides, phrasebooks, emergency numbers and apps, weather forecasts and exchange rates.

The app has no sign-up, login or user account.

3. Services the app contacts

The app has no backend of its own. To work, it sends requests directly from your phone to the services below. Like any internet request, each service can also see your IP address and basic technical details such as your device type and app version. None of these requests includes your name.

Please keep personal details out of the chat

Whatever you type into the place chat is sent to Google Gemini to produce an answer. Do not enter sensitive personal information such as passwords, financial details, health information or your home address.

AI-generated content

Itineraries, descriptions, phrases, emergency numbers and chat answers are generated by an AI model and can be wrong or out of date. Please verify opening hours, prices and emergency numbers with official sources before relying on them.

Links that leave the app

Some buttons open other apps or websites at your request: your maps app (“Directions”), the phone dialer (“Call”) and a website in your browser. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies. The app does not read your location for this: it only passes the place name and address you are looking at.

Speech

“Read aloud” and phrase pronunciation use the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK in the app may collect and send to Google information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location that can be derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app name and version, language, screen size);
  • how ads perform (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data about the ad SDK.

The app does not pass your name, your trips or your chat messages to AdMob.

Personalized and non-personalized ads

By default, ads may be personalized, which means Google can use the advertising ID and similar data to choose ads based on your interests. Non-personalized ads are based on general information such as the content of the app and your approximate location, and not on your past behavior.

Consent in Europe and the UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before ads that use your data are requested. You can change your choice later where the app offers a privacy-options entry, or through your Google account and device settings.

How to limit or turn off ad personalization

  • On Android: open Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads) to opt out of ad personalization, reset your advertising ID, or delete it.
  • In your Google account: manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Turning off personalization does not remove ads; the ads you see will just be less relevant to you.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load content from the services above and to know when you are offline.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by the ad SDK as described in section 4.

The app does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or phone calls. The “Call” button opens your dialer with the number filled in; you decide whether to place the call.

6. How information is used

  • To create and show itineraries, weather, exchange rates, phrases, emergency information and chat answers.
  • To make the app work offline by saving that content on your device.
  • To show and measure advertising, which pays for the app.

I do not run my own analytics or crash-reporting service in the app, and I do not build profiles of users. Google Play may give me aggregated statistics about installs and crashes that do not identify you.

7. Sharing and selling

I do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the services listed in sections 3 and 4, and only as needed to provide the features you use and to show ads. Those services act under their own terms and privacy policies. I may disclose information if required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single trip from the home screen (long-press it, or tap the bin icon), clear a conversation in the chat, or use Profile → Delete saved trips and data to remove all trips, chats and saved offline information. Your name and preferences can be changed in Profile. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on your device.
  • At third parties: I do not control how long Google, Pexels, Open-Meteo or ExchangeRate-API keep the technical data they receive. See their policies linked above. For ad data held by Google, use the controls described in section 4.

9. Security

Requests to the services above are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Data saved on your device is protected by your device’s own security (screen lock and Android’s app sandboxing). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Travel Wizard is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact me and I will help you remove it.

11. International transfers

The services the app uses, including Google, operate globally. Your requests may therefore be processed in countries other than your own, including outside the European Economic Area and the UK. These providers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or other mechanisms described in their own policies.

12. Your rights and choices

Because I do not collect your data on my own servers, most of it is under your direct control on your device (see section 8). Depending on where you live, you may also have rights over data processed by the third parties above, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You can exercise these rights with the relevant provider using the links in this policy, or contact me and I will help where I can. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis (GDPR/UK GDPR): ads that use your data rely on your consent; delivering the features you request relies on performance of the service you asked for and my legitimate interest in running a working app.

California and other US states: I do not sell personal information. Advertising through Google may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some state laws; you can opt out using the ad settings described in section 4.

13. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy, for example when the app adds a feature or a service changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced in the app or in the Google Play listing.
